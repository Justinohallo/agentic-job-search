# Rubric

Score each dimension 1–4 with a quoted line from the answer as evidence. Under 3 earns a drill.

| Dimension | 1 | 2 | 3 | 4 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Mechanism** | Names the answer only | Right answer, vague why | Explains the mechanism when prompted | Explains the mechanism unprompted, at the right depth |
| **Structure** | Rambles | Some order | Clear: answer, reason, example | Answer first, trade-offs named, checks the interviewer is with them |
| **Evidence** | Hypothetical only | Generic past work | A real example | A real example with a MASTER-backed result |
| **Time** | Runs out of time | Over by >25% | On time | On time with a summary |
| **Build** (coding rounds) | Doesn't run | Runs, major gaps | Works, minor gaps | Works, tested edge cases, narrated decisions |

## Drill format (DEFENSE.md)

```
- **Drill <n> (<date>, <round>):** <question>
  - The mechanism in two sentences, from memory: <to fill on each rep>
  - Whiteboard: <what you'd draw to prove it>
  - Reps: <date: pass/fail>
```

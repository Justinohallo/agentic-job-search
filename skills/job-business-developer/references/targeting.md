# Targeting: who gets a message

## Tie-strength bands (F-7)

| Band | Definition | Yield | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Strong | Talked in the last 6 months; close friend or current collaborator | Lower for job mobility than moderate ties, but highest reply rate | Ask for intros to *their* contacts, not for a job |
| **Moderate** | Worked together or met several times; 1–3 years since real contact; some mutual connections | **Highest job mobility** | The default target. Reconnect with a shared detail plus one ask |
| Weak | Met once, same community, or 2nd-degree via a mutual | High mobility, low reply rate | Only with a mutual intro or a very specific shared hook |
| Stranger | No connection | Lowest | Only for a referral ask at an active application, after a mutual was checked |

## Score (0–9)

- Tie: moderate 3, strong 2, weak 2, stranger 0
- Proximity: at an active PIPELINE company 3, at a target company or fintech 2, React/frontend elsewhere 1, other 0
- Can vouch for named work (a MASTER A-id) 3, knows them professionally 1, socially 0

Message the top 10 not contacted in the last 30 days. Ties at the same score go to the person nearest a live role.

## Where contacts come from

1. MASTER: managers, teammates, reports, clients, references (`## References`, role notes, session captures in `career/sources/`).
2. Communities the candidate runs or belongs to (meetups, bootcamp or school alumni).
3. LinkedIn: 1st-degree connections at PIPELINE companies, then 2nd-degree with a named mutual. Read-only lookup; never connect or message.
4. People who already helped (a past referrer): thank them and update them first.

## Asks, ranked by how easy they are to say yes to

1. "Would you refer me for <posting link>?" (they click a button)
2. "Who on your team should I talk to about <posting>?"
3. "Do you know anyone hiring React contractors right now?"
4. "Could we talk for 20 minutes about <company>?"
5. "Would you be a reference?" (only for people who can vouch for a named A-id)
